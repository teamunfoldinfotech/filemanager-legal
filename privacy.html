Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how File Manager (“the App”, package com.bluelight.filemanager.computerstyle.filehandler) handles information when you use the App.

1. Information we process

  • Local files: The App reads and manages files and folders on your device that you choose to browse, open, move, delete, compress, or share.
  • App settings: Preferences such as theme, language, favorites, recent files, app lock, and display options are stored on your device.
  • Cloud accounts (optional): If you sign in to Google Drive or Dropbox, the App accesses files and account information needed to list, upload, download, or manage files in that service, subject to the permissions you grant.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to provide App features you request, including file browsing, media playback, document preview, storage analysis, secure folder, and cloud storage access.

3. Data storage and sharing

  • Local file and preference data stays on your device unless you explicitly share or upload it.
  • Cloud sign-in and file access are handled through the cloud provider (for example Google or Dropbox). Their privacy policies also apply.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

4. Permissions

The App may request storage, network, and biometric/device-credential permissions to manage files, connect to cloud services, and protect the App with App Lock / Secure Folder.

5. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
